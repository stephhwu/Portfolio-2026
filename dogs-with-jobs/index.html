Dogs with Jobs

View Live Site ↗

Industry

  • Data Visualization

Year

2025

A data story of working dogs, examining how their unique abilities remain irreplaceable even in an increasingly technological world.

Services

  • D3.js
  • Vue.js
  • Photoshop

Brief

What began as a co-evolutionary relationship between humans and wolves over 130,000 years ago has given rise to specialized working dog breeds whose deeply entwined skills and sensibilities remain irreplaceable, even in our technological age.

Finding

These aren't just good boys and girls — they co-evolved with us, challenging the very notion of human exceptionalism. Dogs may be the smartest partnership our species has ever made, and it's on us to keep studying them and prioritizing their well-being as their roles keep evolving.

Background

Data pulled from some unlikely sources.

Data was gathered from the American Kennel Club, scientific journals, and the Government Accountability Office, then visualized in D3.js with a Riso-print aesthetic carried consistently across every illustration.

Detail

A palette dogs can actually see.

Dogs see primarily blue and yellow, so those became the project's core palette — and since dogs can't see red or read at all, red text marks information that's irrelevant to them, a tongue-in-cheek nod for the "guide dog" character. Ivy Presto Headline brings a touch of elegance that plays against the lighthearted illustrations, Roca adds a modern serif twist, and Neue Haas Grotesk keeps body copy clean and legible. Four hidden symbols tie back to the four working-dog job categories — guides, blue collar & misc, sniffers, and medical — small easter eggs for anyone paying attention.
