About Steph

With a background in psychology and graphic design, Steph brought these disciplines together during her M.S. in Data Visualization at Parsons School of Design.

She's passionate about working across research, illustration, graphic design, product design, and data visualization.

What excites her most is using design as a tool for impact. For Steph, every project is an opportunity to tell compelling stories through visuals that don't just inform, but inspire action and spark change.

When she's not designing, you'll find her hiking, bouldering, or making pottery.
